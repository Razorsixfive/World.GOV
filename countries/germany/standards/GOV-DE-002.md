# GOV-DE-002: Scheduled Readiness Reporting

Revision: 1.0  
Status: Adopted for the fictional administration  
Review: Maintainer review through a normal pull request

## Purpose

The schedule can be deployed considerably faster than the infrastructure it describes.

## Requirements

A fictional project office MAY publish a revised opening date. It MUST preserve previous announcements in the schedule history rather than treating a changed date as completed construction.

Publishing an announcement MUST NOT set the terminal's construction or acceptance flags. Calendar milestones and physical readiness MUST remain separate. This reporting policy MUST NOT override real engineering checks, approvals, or technical build results.

## Verification

DE-2020-001 records the Terminal 1 opening and distinguishes it from other transfer dates. SRC-DE-005 and SRC-DE-006 support the factual account. Content validation resolves the source and standard references; human review checks the chronology.

## Satirical implementation note

The scheduling component reports zero missed deadlines after replacing its only deadline. The construction component has declined to implement the same reset operation.

## Interpretation

The schedule logic is fictional satire of project reporting. It does not allege misconduct by named people or describe a real airport safety procedure.
