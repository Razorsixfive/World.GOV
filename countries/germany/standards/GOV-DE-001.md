# GOV-DE-001: Provisional Configuration Lifecycle

Revision: 1.0  
Status: Adopted for the fictional administration  
Review: Maintainer review through a normal pull request

## Purpose

A provisional label may remain in production long enough to become its most permanent field.

## Requirements

The fictional configuration registry MAY retain a provisional label without an automatic expiry timer. It MUST record the distinction between a lifecycle label and the guarantees provided by the configuration.

Constitutional protections MUST NOT be represented as optional trial features. Renaming a configuration MUST NOT silently disable its guarantees. Historical accounts of adoption and later constitutional continuity MUST remain sourced separately from this metaphor.

## Verification

This is a fictional software-policy joke about provisional naming, not a German legal standard or a claim that constitutional rights were provisional in force.
