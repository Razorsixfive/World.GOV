# GOV-020: Paper Preparedness Accounting

Revision: 1.0  
Status: Adopted for the fictional administration  
Review: Maintainer review through a normal pull request

## Purpose

A delivery receipt is easier to obtain than evidence of preparedness.

## Requirements

The fictional dashboard MAY promote envelopes delivered into households prepared. Its audit copy MUST retain what was actually counted. A letterbox MUST NOT be invited to certify comprehension.

The real project MUST distinguish distribution, readership, understanding, and practical readiness. Evidence for one MUST NOT be presented as evidence for the others. Historical emergency guidance MUST be described in its original context; reproducing a source does not endorse its advice for present-day use.

## Verification

The `DK-1962-001` record links the original leaflet and the Civil Defence memorandum through published transcriptions. Content validation checks references and archive structure. A reviewer checks the account and its limits against the sources; no automated test measures historical public preparedness.

## Interpretation

Government behavior described here is fictional. Engineering and historical-integrity requirements govern the real project. Amendment requires a revised document and ordinary code review.
