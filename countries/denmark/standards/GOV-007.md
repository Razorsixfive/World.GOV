## Purpose

A document may become easier to approve as its visible content decreases.

## Requirements

A fictional redaction MUST be labeled satire. Factual revisions MUST use present-day commits and preserve historical event dates.

An optional `satire.archive` memo MUST use a `SIM-ARCHIVE-` identifier and start in redacted mode. Visitors MAY reveal its full fictional text and restore its black bars. Source links, historical accounts, and uncertainty MUST remain visible in both modes. The administration MAY retain obsolete redactions to preserve the document's atmosphere.

The fictional stamp MAY progress from CLASSIFIED to DECLASSIFIED and back to RECLASSIFIED. Optional state messages SHOULD develop an incident-specific joke; restoring secrecy MAY leave museum opening hours unaffected. These presentation states MUST NOT imply a real classification decision.

Archive text is public static-site data. The toggle is a joke about presentation, not a security control or a claim of access to classified documents. Do not add actual sensitive material, invented archival originals, or unsupported declassification dates. Keep public archive translations aligned with the English memo's identifier, line order, and redaction flags.

## Verification

`VirtualHistoryIsStableAndExplicitlyFictional` verifies that simulated commits are labeled. `ArchiveStartsRedactedAndCanDisplayTheFullMemo` checks the memo's initial presentation and full-text view. `ArchiveTranslationsPreserveTheCanonicalStructure` rejects translated redaction changes. Source accuracy still requires editorial review.
