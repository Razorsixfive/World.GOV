# GOV-HIST-001: Historical Source Integrity

Revision: 1.1  
Status: Adopted for the fictional administration  
Review: Maintainer review through a normal pull request

## Purpose

The evidence department is exempt from the joke.

## Requirements

Every published incident MUST have credible sources supporting its claims. Uncertain, disputed, and interpretive accounts MUST explain their limitations. Fiction MUST NOT be supplied as evidence.

Evidence status MUST describe the historical claim accurately:

- **Documented:** the cited evidence directly supports the stated account; this does not settle every broader interpretation.
- **Interpretive:** attribute the explanation or relationship to a named author and state its scope and limits.
- **Uncertain:** identify the material unknown and its effect on the account or date; do not present a sorting estimate as an exact date.
- **Disputed:** identify what is contested, summarize credible competing accounts, and cite sources for those accounts. Keep established facts separate from the disagreement.

## Verification

MissingSourcesPreventExport and UncertainYearKeepsPrecisionAndRequiresExplanation enforce structural checks; source accuracy requires human review.
