# GOV-021: Reservations Survive Formatting

Revision: 1.0  
Status: Adopted for the fictional administration  
Review: Maintainer review through a normal pull request

## Purpose

A smaller font does not make a disagreement smaller.

## Requirements

The fictional executive summary MAY report agreement above the fold while keeping the qualification below it. The full record MUST preserve the reservation. Restoring the black bars MAY improve the headline's appearance without changing the underlying position.

The real historical account and its translations MUST retain material reservations and their scope. A reservation concerning a specific NATO position MUST NOT be described as withdrawal from the alliance. Historical footnotes MUST remain accessible in the linked evidence; the fictional dossier is not their transcript.

## Verification

The `DK-1982-001` record links a historical account of the parliamentary mechanism and a published NATO communiqué with an explicit reservation. Content validation checks the references; a reviewer checks the wording against both sources.

## Interpretation

Government behavior described here is fictional. Engineering and historical-integrity requirements govern the real project. Amendment requires a revised document and ordinary code review.
