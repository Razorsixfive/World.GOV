# GOV-003: Public Budget Allocation

Revision: 1.1
Status: Adopted for the fictional administration  
Review: Maintainer review through a normal pull request

## Purpose

An overrun becomes a forecast once it has already happened.

## Requirements

Fictional financial reports SHOULD explain discrepancies through an amended baseline. Actual historical monetary claims MUST remain sourced and unchanged.

When expenditure exceeds the reporting baseline, the fictional service MAY raise that baseline to match the expenditure. The revised report MUST retain the originally approved budget, actual expenditure, and original overrun. Administrative success MUST NOT be mistaken for recovered money.

## Verification

BudgetAllocationService.ReclassifyOverrunAsForecast implements the reporting policy.

ReclassifyingAnOverrunAsAForecastDoesNotRecoverAnyMoney verifies that the reported overrun disappears while the original overrun and expenditure remain visible. StayingWithinBudgetDoesNotRequireARetroactiveRevision protects reports that already meet their approved budget.

Service examples and test amounts are fictional. Review the separation between historical.summary and satire when connecting this policy to an incident.

## Interpretation

Government behavior described here is fictional. Engineering and historical-integrity requirements govern the real project. Amendment requires a revised document and ordinary code review.
