# Code of Conduct

World.GOV contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful and constructive.

Examples of behavior that supports the project include:

- Treating people with respect across backgrounds and experience levels.
- Critiquing historical claims, code, and institutions without harassing contributors.
- Keeping satire directed at systems and decisions rather than protected groups or private individuals.
- Reporting safety concerns privately to the repository maintainers.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, hateful language, threats, doxxing, and repeated disruption of review. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces used for project work.
